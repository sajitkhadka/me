**Status:** open
**Type:** epic
**Created:** 2026-10-12

## Description

The site still says "Open to New Opportunities / Available Now". The resume shows old jobs and dates, and a phone number is public. The apps list has dead entries and misses most shipped projects. This epic brings content, design and code up to date.

Goal: a visitor sees who Sajit is today (Software Engineer, Integrations at Perceptyx, runs a home k3s cluster), sees real projects, and the site is easy to keep current.

Non-goals: hosting changes (see [0022](0022-self-host-on-k3s.md)) and new blog features.

Do not publish confidential employer details: no colleague names, no reorg news, no contract dates, no customer names or sizes. Ask before naming internal systems.

Sources for current facts: `D:\projects\career` (history) and the English trainer `data/profile.md` (current work).
